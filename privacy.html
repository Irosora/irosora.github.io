Workbench Personal – Privacy Policy

Workbench Personal is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's Gmail (read-only) and reads and edits the owner's Google Sheets. This data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google account. It is not collected, stored on other servers, shared, or sold.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
